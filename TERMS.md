## Downloading responsibly

Vireo can save the video, audio and files that a page you are viewing already plays or offers, so you can watch them
offline. **You are responsible for what you download and how you use it.** Only download what you have the right to:

- your own content, or content you have permission to save;
- content released under a licence that allows copies (Creative Commons and similar);
- content the site's own terms allow you to download.

Many sites' terms of service do not allow downloading. Respect them, and respect the people who made what you watch.

and refused. Vireo does not include, and will not accept contributions of, any means to get around it.
