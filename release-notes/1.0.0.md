The first release of Vireo: a fast, private browser for Windows, with no ads, no pop-ups, and one-click saving of the videos
and files you watch.

### Browse clean

- Ads and trackers blocked before they load (EasyList, EasyPrivacy, Peter Lowe's and AdGuard's lists, kept up to date),
  pop-up windows and surprise new tabs stopped, leftover ad boxes hidden. Blocking can be turned off for one site.
- Clean links (tracking tags removed), secure connections preferred, your network address hidden from video-call scripts,
  and Microsoft Defender SmartScreen protection for pages and downloads.
- Private windows that leave nothing behind but the files you choose to keep. The first private download asks where
  private downloads should go.

### Save what you watch

- A Download button on every video and audio player, listing its qualities, and a toolbar button counting what the page
  offers.
- Plain files, HLS and DASH streams, live streams (recorded until you stop), and videos whose picture and sound come
  separately, joined into one MP4 by Vireo itself. Where a site offers a video in two codecs, Vireo picks H.264, which
  plays on every computer, wherever it has the size.
- Protected (DRM) content is recognised and never downloaded.
- A download manager that splits big files over several connections and survives a dropped connection or a restart.

### Light and yours

- Hidden tabs fall asleep and wake with their page; built for laptops with 4 GB of memory as much as fast desktops.
- Tabs on any side, drag a tab out into its own window or onto another window's tabs, and a Tabs panel (Ctrl+Shift+A) to
  search every open tab.
- Pinned sites on the start page, separate profiles, and sync through a folder in your own cloud storage, encrypted with
  your passphrase.
- Updates download in the background, are checked against the checksum published with the release, and install when you
  close Vireo. No administrator prompts.

**Requirements:** 64-bit Windows 10 (1809 or later) or Windows 11. Setup installs Microsoft's WebView2 engine if the
computer does not have it yet.
