Welcome to Vireo

A fast, private browser with a real download manager inside. Here is what it does, and three things to set up.

Ads and trackers blocked Pages load faster and follow you less. Built in, nothing to install.
Save videos from pages Press Save video on a page that plays one, in the quality you want.
Downloads that finish Many connections at once, and they carry on after a break or a restart.
